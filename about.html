About Our Farm Shop

Our Story

Highland Harvest Farm Shop is a small local farm shop in Scotland. Our aim is to offer fresh food and support local farmers. We want to make local food easy to find and buy. We work with Scottish farmers and small local producers.

Our Values

Our main values are quality, local food and supporting Scottish farmers. We also try to reduce waste and protect the environment.
