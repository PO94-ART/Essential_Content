# Website Plan

## Website subject

Highland Harvest Farm Shop – a small local farm shop in Scotland

## Purpose

The website will provide information about the farm shop, its products, activities and contact details.

## Target users

Local people, families and visitors who want to buy fresh local food.

## Required pages

| Page | Filename | Planned content |
|---|---|---|
| Home | index.html | Introduction to the farm shop, local food and key information |
| About | about.html | Background, purpose and values of the farm shop |
| Activities | activities.html | Products, seasonal activities and farm shop events |
| Contact | contact.html | Address, opening times, email and travel information |

## Shared content

Navigation menu, farm shop name, footer and the same colours and design on every page.

## Colour ideas

Dark green, light green and cream.

## Website structure

Users can move between all pages by clicking the main navigation menu at the top of the website.
